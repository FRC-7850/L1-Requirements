# Store L1 Requirements here

Team objective:
Create a robot to compete in the 2025 Minnetrials competition - Popcorn Premiere

1. Score popcorn kernels
2. Grab kernels from the snack bar
2a. Grab kernels from the floor
3. drive over a 2 inch barrier
4. Shoot kernels into kettle (48" tall, 24" wide)
5. Hold 16 kernels at one time
6. Pick up butter pieces from the floor
7. place butter in the melter
8. hold 1 butter at a time
9. Raise butter 10"
10. Be able to get into seat
